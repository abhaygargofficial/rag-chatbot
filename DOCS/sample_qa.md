# Sample Q&A — MF FAQ Assistant

## Factual Questions

### Q1: What is the expense ratio of HDFC Flexi Cap Fund?

**Answer:** The Total Expense Ratio (TER) of HDFC Flexi Cap Fund Direct Plan is approximately 0.71% (as per the latest TER report). This includes management fees, administrative costs, and other operational expenses.

**Source:** [HDFC Fund TER Reports](https://www.hdfcfund.com/statutory-disclosure/total-expense-ratio-of-mutual-fund-schemes/reports)

---

### Q2: What is the ELSS lock-in period?

**Answer:** ELSS (Equity Linked Savings Scheme) funds have a mandatory lock-in period of 3 years. This is the shortest lock-in among all tax-saving instruments under Section 80C of the Income Tax Act.

**Source:** [HDFC ELSS Tax Saver Overview](https://www.hdfcfund.com/product-solutions/overview/hdfc-elss-tax-saver/direct)

---

### Q3: What is the minimum SIP amount?

**Answer:** The minimum SIP amount varies by scheme. For most HDFC mutual fund schemes, the minimum SIP investment starts at Rs. 100 per month. Please check the specific scheme page for exact details.

**Source:** [HDFC Flexi Cap Fund](https://www.hdfcfund.com/explore/mutual-funds/hdfc-flexi-cap-fund/direct)

---

### Q4: What is the exit load for HDFC Small Cap Fund?

**Answer:** The exit load for HDFC Small Cap Fund is 1% if redeemed within 1 year of investment. After 1 year, there is no exit load.

**Source:** [HDFC Small Cap Fund](https://www.hdfcfund.com/explore/mutual-funds/hdfc-small-cap-fund/direct)

---

### Q5: How do I download my capital gains statement?

**Answer:** You can download your capital gains statement by:
1. Visiting the HDFC Fund website
2. Logging into your account
3. Navigating to Services > Request Statement
4. Selecting "Capital Gains Statement" and the relevant period

Alternatively, you can request it via WhatsApp or online services.

**Source:** [Capital Gains Statement Guide](https://www.hdfcfund.com/learn/blog/how-get-capital-gain-statement-mutual-fund-schemes-india)

---

### Q6: What is the riskometer of HDFC Flexi Cap Fund?

**Answer:** HDFC Flexi Cap Fund is classified as "Very High Risk" on the AMFI riskometer scale. This is because it invests across large-cap, mid-cap, and small-cap stocks, which are subject to market volatility.

**Source:** [AMFI Riskometer](https://www.amfiindia.com/online-center/risk-o-meter)

---

### Q7: What is the benchmark for HDFC Flexi Cap Fund?

**Answer:** HDFC Flexi Cap Fund uses the Nifty 500 TRI (Total Return Index) as its benchmark. The fund aims to generate long-term capital appreciation by investing across market capitalizations.

**Source:** [HDFC Flexi Cap Fund](https://www.hdfcfund.com/explore/mutual-funds/hdfc-flexi-cap-fund/direct)

---

### Q8: How do I request an account statement?

**Answer:** You can request an account statement through:
1. **Online:** Log in to HDFC Fund website > Services > Request Statement
2. **WhatsApp:** Send a request to the official HDFC Fund WhatsApp number
3. **Email:** Send a request to the customer service email

**Source:** [Request Statement](https://www.hdfcfund.com/services/additional-info/request-statement)
